About us

Diamond Pearl Hotel & Residence is a modern and comfortable hotel in Phnom Penh, offering a relaxing stay for both business and leisure travelers. Conveniently located in the vibrant Diamond Island (Koh Pich) area, the hotel provides easy access to shopping malls, restaurants, entertainment venues, business districts, and major attractions in the city.
