About Our Hotel

D

iamond Pearl Hotel & Residence is a modern and comfortable hotel in Phnom Penh, offering a relaxing stay for both business and leisure travelers. Conveniently located in the vibrant Diamond Island (Koh Pich) area, the hotel provides easy access to shopping malls, restaurants, entertainment venues, business districts, and major attractions in the city.

The hotel features well-appointed rooms and suites designed with comfort and convenience in mind. Guests can enjoy modern amenities, complimentary Wi-Fi, comfortable bedding, private bathrooms, and a welcoming atmosphere throughout their stay.
