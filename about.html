A NEW VISION OF COMFORT

Modern Elegance in Phnom Penh’s Most Vibrant Island

Diamond Pearl Hotel & Residence is a premier destination offering a serene, luxurious haven for both business executives and leisure travelers in Phnom Penh. Situated in the prestigious Koh Pich (Diamond Island) development, our hotel combines prime accessibility with the tranquil ambiance of a riverside retreat.

From our thoughtfully appointed rooms with panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows to our sparkling rooftop swimming pool and authentic gourmet restaurant, every aspect of our property has been curated to deliver unmatched comfort, warm Cambodian hospitality, and seamless convenience.

Whether you are visiting for an international summit, a relaxing weekend getaway, or an extended stay, our dedicated 24-hour concierge and guest services team are committed to ensuring your time with us is truly exceptional.
